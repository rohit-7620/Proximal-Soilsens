Our Story

Proximal SoilSens® was founded by a team of researchers from IIT Bombay with a vision to revolutionize agriculture through cutting-edge technology. As a pioneer in precision agriculture, we specialize in developing innovative solutions for soil health management, water conservation, and sustainable farming.

Our advanced technologies, including the NutriSensX™ Lab-on-the-Farm soil health management system, TerraPort™ – an on-the-go portable moisture meter, and Terra Station, empower farmers with precise, real-time data to optimize resource utilization and enhance productivity.

Impact Achievement: SoilSens has significantly impacted the agricultural sector by improving efficiency and sustainability, with our solutions enabling up to 27% reduction in water usage and 40% decrease in fertilizer application.

Through strategic collaborations with agri-enterprises, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and foundations, we continue to extend our reach and drive meaningful change in the farming community. Proudly "Made in India, Made for the World," SoilSens is transforming agriculture globally across eight countries.

Our Mission

Vision for the Future

Aligning with the Government of India's vision of "Per drop more crop" and inspired by the motto "Jai Jawaan, Jai Kisaan, Jai Vigyaan, Jai Anusandhan," SoilSens is committed to advancing research and innovation to empower farmers.

By integrating precision irrigation with soil health technologies like NutriSensX™, we make farming more efficient, sustainable, and profitable. Our mission is to bridge scientific advancements with grassroots agriculture, providing affordable, data-driven solutions that enhance productivity while conserving precious resources.
