The TerraPort™ Solution

We developed TerraPort™ — a patent-pending, compact, and portable soil moisture meter that allows farmers to measure soil moisture at multiple depths and multiple locations across the field.

This enables the creation of a comprehensive moisture profile, helping farmers make accurate, data-driven irrigation decisions.

Cost-effective, easy to use, and shareable — TerraPort™ can be used across multiple plots or by multiple farmers within a community.
