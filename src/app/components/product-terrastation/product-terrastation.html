SOILSENS® TERRASTATION™

SoilSens® TerraStation™ is a solar powered IoT device that can be customized as per the client needs. It uses an FDR soil moisture probe designed and developed at IIT Bombay — which has gone through extensive testing in the lab and also on the field. It measures soil moisture, soil temperature, ambient humidity, and ambient temperature. It is modular, its height is adjustable, and data can be recorded as per the customer requirement.

How It Works

TerraStation™ is installed in the field and continuously monitors soil and environmental conditions. Data is transmitted wirelessly via IoT to the cloud, where advanced analytics generate actionable advisories sent directly to the farmer's mobile device.

01
Sense

FDR probe and environmental sensors collect real-time data 24/7

02
Transmit

Solar-powered IoT module sends data securely to the cloud

03
Analyze

AI-driven analytics process data and generate precise advisories

04
Advise

Farmer receives personalized irrigation and crop management alerts
