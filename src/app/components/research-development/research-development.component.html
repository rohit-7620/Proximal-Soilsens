Research & Academic Collaborations

Our story began at IIT Bombay, where our passion for soil sensing took shape. We hold an exclusive license for the initial soil moisture sensor technology, and every enhancement since then has been developed independently by our team.

From early prototypes built at CEN, IITBNF, and INUP to field trials through the NNETRA program, collaboration has always been part of our journey.

We're excited to grow this spirit further and welcome research partners, co-funded innovation projects, and on-ground implementation teams who share our mission of bringing cutting-edge agricultural sensing to every farmer.
