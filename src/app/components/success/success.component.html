Case Study 1
Grapes · Nashik

Boosting Grape Yield with SoilSens-NutriSensX™

CD

Mr. Chetan Dattatrya Deshmukh

📍 Rajapur, Dindori Tehsil, Nasik District

🍇 Grape Farmer · 15+ years · 10 Acres

📖 Background

Mr. Chetan Dattatrya Deshmukh has been farming grapes for over 15 years. His vineyard spans 10 acres, and like many farmers in the region, he faced difficulties in maintaining optimal soil nutrient levels, particularly during the critical fruiting stage. Traditional soil testing methods were either too slow or too costly.

⚠️ Challenges
  • Delayed or inaccurate soil nutrient data, especially potassium levels crucial for grape production
  • Risk of reduced crop yields due to nutrient deficiencies
  • Potential income loss due to suboptimal fertilizer application timing
💡 Solution

In January 2024, Mr. Deshmukh adopted the SoilSens NutriSensX™ — compact, easy to use, and requires no electricity, making it ideal for on-field use. The device provided accurate, real-time data on soil nutrients, particularly potassium, which is critical during the fruiting stage of grape cultivation.

🔧 Implementation
  1. Integrated SoilSens into his farming routine
  2. Conducted a soil test during the critical fruiting stage of his grape crop
  3. Received instant and accurate soil test results directly at the vineyard
  4. Identified a potassium deficiency in the soil
  5. Immediately applied the recommended amount of potash based on the soil nutrient data
Results
✅
Improved Crop Yield

Significantly better grape yield by addressing potassium deficiency promptly.

💰
Prevented Income Loss

Saved ₹60,000–₹70,000 per crop through timely potash application.

🌱
Optimized Fertilizer Use

Precise timing enhanced overall soil health and productivity.

"

Using SoilSens has been incredibly beneficial for my vineyard. The instant soil data during the fruiting stage helped me identify a potassium deficiency, allowing me to apply potash immediately. This timely action resulted in a significantly better grape yield. If I had waited or missed this, it would have severely impacted my income. I highly recommend SoilSens to all farmers looking to improve their crop management and profitability.

— Mr. Chetan Dattatrya Deshmukh

Case Study 3
Onion · Ahmednagar

Cutting Fertilizer Costs by 40% with NutriSensX™

GM

Mr. Ganesh Mahandule

📍 Village Mahandulwadi, Taluka Ahmednagar, Maharashtra

🧅 Onion Farmer · 1 Acre

📖 Background

Ganesh Mahandule has been farming Onion for the last few years. His field spans one acre. Like many farmers in the region, he faced difficulties maintaining soil health and optimizing fertilizer usage. Traditional testing methods were either too expensive or inaccessible.

⚠️ Challenges
  • Overuse of fertilizers leading to increased costs and soil degradation
  • Inconsistent crop yields and reduced productivity
  • Lack of access to timely and accurate soil health data
💡 Solution

In May 2023, Ganesh Mahandule adopted NutriSensX™. The device provided accurate, real-time data on soil nutrients, particularly phosphorus levels critical for crop growth. He reduced Nitrogen/Urea excess and applied the recommended Phosphorus and Potassium amounts. His fertilizer spend dropped from ₹8,800/acre to ₹5,350/acre — saving ₹3,450/acre.

🔧 Implementation
  1. Integrated SoilSens into his regular farming routine
  2. Conducted soil tests before planting and after each harvest
  3. Adjusted fertilizer application based on NutriSensX™ soil health report
  4. Monitored soil health regularly to make informed decisions
Results
