Directly in Your Field

SoilSens® was born at IIT Bombay with a singular mission: bring lab-grade soil intelligence directly to the farmer's field. Our patented electrochemical sensing technology and IoT ecosystem make precision agriculture accessible, affordable, and actionable — at every pincode in India and beyond.

From portable handheld devices to cloud-connected monitoring stations and AI-powered analytics platforms, SoilSens delivers a complete 360° agri-tech ecosystem that transforms raw field data into decisions that grow better crops, conserve resources, and build sustainable livelihoods.
